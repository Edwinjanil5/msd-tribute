Biography & Career

Mahendra Singh Dhoni

Born on July 7, 1981, in Ranchi (now in Jharkhand), M.S. Dhoni rose from working as a Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) at Kharagpur railway station to becoming India's most successful white-ball captain.

Early Life & Rise to International Cricket

Before focusing on cricket, Dhoni played football as a goalkeeper and badminton at the school level in Ranchi. Encouraged by his school coach to try wicketkeeping, he quickly impressed in domestic cricket for Bihar and Jharkhand.

He made his One Day International (ODI) debut for India in December 2004 against Bangladesh, and soon caught global attention with explosive innings such as his 148 against Pakistan and unbeaten 183 against Sri Lanka in 2005.

Captaincy & Major ICC Trophies

Known worldwide as "Captain Cool" for his calm decision-making under pressure, Dhoni remains the only captain in cricket history to win all three major ICC white-ball tournaments:

2007 ICC World Twenty20

Led a young Indian squad to victory in the inaugural T20 World Cup in South Africa.

2011 ICC Cricket World Cup

Ended India's 28-year wait for an ODI World Cup title with an unbeaten 91* in the final at Mumbai.

2013 ICC Champions Trophy

Defeated England in the final in Birmingham to complete the historic ICC white-ball treble.

Test Mace & IPL Legacy

Guided India to the No. 1 ICC Test ranking in 2009 and captained Chennai Super Kings (CSK) to five IPL titles.

Honors & Legacy

  • Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (2007–08): India's highest sporting honor.
  • Padma Shri (2009) & Padma Bhushan (2018): Prestigious civilian awards from the Government of India.
  • Territorial Army: Conferred the honorary rank of Lieutenant Colonel in the Indian Territorial Army in 2011.
  • International Retirement: Announced his retirement from international cricket on August 15, 2020, while continuing to feature in the IPL.
